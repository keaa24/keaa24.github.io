Privacy Policy for GPS Toolbox

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy describes how Duman Emre Akın's Android app GPS Toolbox (com.emre.gpstoolbox) accesses, uses, stores, and shares data.

Location data and how we use it

GPS Toolbox requests approximate and precise location permissions. With your permission, the app accesses location readings from Android, including latitude, longitude, accuracy, and time, as well as altitude, speed, and direction of travel when available. Features such as route recording and saving your current position use precise location.

  • The app uses location to show your position, GPS measurements, and directions to saved places.
  • When you record a route or measure by walking, the app uses location points to draw the route and calculate distance or area.
  • When you save a place, a parking spot, a return point, or a location alarm, the app stores its coordinates along with any name or note you enter.
  • When you take a GPS photo, the app uses the device's camera app to take the picture and adds coordinates, time, accuracy, and altitude when available. It stores the original and stamped copies on your device.

Background location access

If you turn on automatic route recording, start a manual route, or activate a location alarm, GPS Toolbox can access precise location in the background, including when the screen is locked or you are using another app. It uses these readings to continue a route or check whether you enter or leave the area you selected for the alarm. Automatic route recording is off by default. The tracking service uses an ongoing notification, subject to your Android notification settings.

If you grant Android's Allow all the time location permission, the app can resume automatic recording, an unpaused manual route, or an active location alarm after the device restarts, before you reopen the app. You can turn off automatic recording, end a manual route, disable an alarm, or change location access in Android settings.

When information leaves your device

  • Online maps. When you enable online maps, the app requests map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation over HTTPS. The service receives your IP address, request details, and tile coordinates that indicate the map area and zoom level you are viewing. That area may include your current or saved location. Tile requests do not attach your saved routes, notes, or photos. OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • Sharing and directions. When you choose to share coordinates, a GPX route, or a GPS-stamped photo, the app passes that information to the app you choose. A GPX file includes route points and their timestamps, with altitude when available. When you open directions in a navigation app, it receives the selected destination coordinates. The recipient's own privacy practices apply.
  • Support email. If you contact the developer, the developer receives your email address and the information you include, and uses it to respond to your inquiry.

GPS Toolbox does not require an account and does not upload saved records to a developer server. The app does not use location for advertising or analytics.

Storage, retention, and deletion

GPS Toolbox stores its records and photos in app-private storage protected by Android's app isolation. The app disables Android's app backup for its data. Map requests use HTTPS.

  • Automatic location history shows the most recent 24 hours. The app removes older history points when it starts or the tracking service performs cleanup.
  • Saved manual and automatic routes, saved places, measurements, and alarm records are separate from the 24-hour history. They remain on the device until you delete them or clear the app's storage.
  • Use Settings → Data and about → Clear recent location history to delete recent location points. You can delete saved records individually. Deleting a photo entry removes its record. To remove its original and stamped files from the app's storage, clear GPS Toolbox storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Clearing app storage also removes local records and cached files. Copies you already shared or exported are managed by the recipient or the app where you saved them.

Children's privacy

GPS Toolbox is not directed to children under the age of 13.
